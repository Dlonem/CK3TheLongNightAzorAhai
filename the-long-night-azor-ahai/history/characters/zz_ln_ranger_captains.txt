# AGOT: The Long Night & Azor Ahai -- seven generations of ranging captains.
#
# WHY HISTORY CHARACTERS AND NOT SCRIPT. The engine builds a domicile exactly once per title: at game start,
# for a title whose history gives it a holder -- the same way AGOT's First Ranger gets his camp
# (history/titles/agot_laamp_titles.txt:1477). change_government at runtime does not build camps; two live
# saves proved it. So the companies ride from the first day of every bookmark, captained by these men, and
# the camps their titles carry outlive every one of them.
#
# Field-for-field on AGOT's own 00_agot_char_nightswatch.txt, including their
# agot_join_nightswatch_history_effect (ORDER = ranger) -- reference to their effect, no AGOT file touched.
