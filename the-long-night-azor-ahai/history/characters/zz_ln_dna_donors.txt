# these, Dummy_Scaly_1 and Dummy_Eunuch_1 among them, each born and dead a day later in year 118 and read at
# runtime by 00_agot_canon_children_effects.txt. The Night King's own face already comes off a donor the same
# way (others_thenightking, in othersinvasion_characters.txt) -- this is that, for everyone else.
#
# He is dead before any bookmark, holds nothing, is in nobody's court and has no father, so he cannot appear
# in a game. If the engine ever prunes him anyway the copy is skipped and reported rather than failing --
# see ln_wear_the_walkers_face_effect.
