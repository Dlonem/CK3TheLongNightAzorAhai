# AGOT: The Long Night & Azor Ahai -- decision titles that know where you are standing (1.1.0, §8.2).
#
# Thomas: "if you are a commander in an army, the text on the decision itself changes to 'Send a raven'...
# you can't send a raven to Forge Lightbringer, but perhaps you can send a raven to empty your dungeons."
#
# THIS IS HALF OF A TWO-PART CHANGE and neither half works alone: the gate swap
# (is_available_adult_or_is_commanding, marked §8.2 at each decision) lets the decision through while
# commanding; these tokens rename it while he is in the field. Called from loc as
# [ROOT.Char.Custom('token')]. This is the mod's first file in this folder -- customlockcheck.py (gate)
# resolves every token and every localization_key in it.
#
# ⚠ NOT trigger_localization. Different folder, different shape, different job (§8's two halves).
