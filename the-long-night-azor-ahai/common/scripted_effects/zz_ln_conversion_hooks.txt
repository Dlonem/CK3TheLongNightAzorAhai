# WHAT THIS IS. A place for another mod to say what should happen to a character at the moment the
# dead take them, without having to shadow agot_long_night_scripted_effects.txt -- a 5,000-line file
# that would go stale the day this mod updates.
#
# On its own this does nothing at all. Vanilla ships empty scripted effects for exactly this purpose
# (game/common/scripted_effects/00_camp_officer_effects.txt:131), so an empty body is the supported
# form and costs nothing per call.
#
# WHO OVERRIDES IT TODAY. The AGOT: Supernatural & The Long Night Compatibility Patch, to unmake
# whatever a character was before the Others got the body -- a vampire raised as a wight would
# otherwise keep its immortality, its thirst events and its trait track while standing in the Night
# King's line.
#
# WHERE IT IS CALLED. ln_mutate_into_other_unguarded_effect, next to the dragonglass strip and
# DELIBERATELY OUTSIDE the one-shot has-wight_trait guard, so the conversion's re-runs on wights that
# already exist in a running save reach it too (the title-gain and liege-culture hooks in
# 00_others_on_actions.txt re-run the whole conversion over a dead ruler's court when he gains land;
# the heartbeat does not -- its limits skip wight_trait). That means it can fire more than once per
# wight: any override must open with a single cheap trigger and fall straight through for the
# ordinary corpse, which is all of them.
