# The Night's Watch

*Part of the [AGOT: The Long Night & Azor Ahai](Home) wiki. On dlonem.com: [Take the black, and mean it](https://dlonem.com/mods/the-long-night/#invasion).*

The Watch is the part of the mod the showcase video left out, because it would have doubled the runtime. It is a complete second way to play the Long Night — from the Wall, with no lands and no heir to protect.

## Taking the black

**Take the Black** lays down your lands, hands the realm to your heir, and puts you on the Wall. Your children and close family pass to the heir; they do not follow you. From there you can hold one of the Wall's castles as a sworn brother, or ride out as a ranging captain. The Watch is brothers only: a woman is refused at the gate rather than admitted and deposed.

Sworn brothers cannot inherit or keep southern lands or the claims on them, cannot end up regent of a southern kingdom, and cannot hold a place under one of the dead — nor can one of the dead hold a place in the Watch.

## The ranging companies

Eight landless companies, each with a real camp on the map from day one: **the Long Ranging, the Deep, the Cold, the Silent, the Black, the Winter, the Far and the Last.** A ranging captain is landless and oathbound, moves his camp the way the First Ranger moves his, and competes with the other captains for **First Ranger**. Beyond the Wall, in *Winter Is Coming*, a ranging is also where you are likeliest to meet a lone White Walker before the Long Night begins.

The *Ranging Companies* game rule decides whether the Watch fills the companies itself when men run short (the default), leaves them for players, or sends all eight out from the start.

## The castles

Hold a Wall castle as its keeper. Manning an empty castle puts brothers into one that stands unmanned; restoring a ruined one rebuilds it. The Nightfort comes last in the ladder of keeperships, because granting it hands over the whole county.

## The Lord Commander

**Call the Great Ranging.** Two hundred brothers, provisioned for a season, on the best horses the Watch has left — sent north for word of what is out there. What comes back depends on whether the Lord Commander rode with them. Faster to call again in *Winter Is Coming*, and faster still in the Long Night.

**Send a Brother Recruiting.** A sworn brother goes gate to gate down the length of Westeros, asking every great lord the old question: how full are your dungeons, and what were you planning to do with the men inside them? Each lord answers in his own way, and the Watch remembers who fed it.

## What southern lords can do

**Send Men and Grain.** A cart, a season's stores, or your whole vault. The Watch keeps a list, and it decides what it will do for you later.

**Send the Dungeons to the Wall.** One decision, every prisoner who can take the black. The tooltip tells you how many will go before you click, and names the reason for each one who cannot. It is an order, not an offer. The *Send the Prisoners* game rule sets whether the Wall takes counts and below or any rank.

**Ask for a Castle**, **Ask for a Command**, **Return to the Wall**, **March the Ranging**, and a petition to the realm — the Watch has decisions of its own for when the castles stand empty and the realm has stopped listening.

## The Watch and the Long Night

The Wall's fall is a scripted collapse at Castle Black, and a sworn brother reads the monument card thirty days later in his own voice. When the dark lifts, the **Question of the North** asks the crown whether the Night's Watch is restored or the Gift opened to the free folk — and the Watch is only refounded if it is genuinely gone. If wildlings hold Castle Black at the Dawn, that is a thing they did.
