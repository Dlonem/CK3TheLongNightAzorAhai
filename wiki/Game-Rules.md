# Game Rules

*Part of the [AGOT: The Long Night & Azor Ahai](Home) wiki. On dlonem.com: [Your apocalypse, your rules](https://dlonem.com/mods/the-long-night/#rules).*

The mod adds thirteen game rules. Every one of them is baked in when a save is created, so changing a rule means a new campaign. Defaults are marked.

## The invasion

| Rule | Options |
|---|---|
| **Chance** | **Certain** (default) · Near-Certain · Even Odds · Remote Odds · No Chance |
| **Moment** | **A Random Summer** (default) · Immediately · In Fifty Years · In One Hundred Years · In One Hundred Fifty Years · Historical · Full Random · Manual (only when you take the decision) |
| **Strength** | Weak · **Standard** (default) · Strong · Insane |
| **War Tier** | Duchy (Slow Grind) · **March** (Recommended, default) · Empire (Rapid Onslaught) |
| **Resolution** | War for the Dawn · **Death** (default) |
| **His Dragon** | **They Raise Their Kills** (default) · One Simply Appears · Off |
| **Iron Throne Reformation** | **On** (default) · Off |
| **Recurrence** | **Once** (default) · The Cycle |

### What the strength rule actually changes

Each tier sets the Night King's own combat modifiers, the size his host grows to, and — on Strong and Insane — how fast the dead move.

| | Weak | Standard | Strong | Insane |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Advantage | 10 | 15 | 20 | 20 |
| Army toughness | — | +10% | +20% | +35% |
| Army damage | — | — | +15% | +30% |
| Knight effectiveness | — | — | — | +25% |
| Host, top of the ladder | 187k | 250k | 312k | 375k |
| Pause between won wars | 2–6 months | 2–6 months | 1–3 months | 1–3 months |
| Freeze after a lost war | 1–3 years | 1–3 years | 6–18 months | 6–18 months |
| Extra duchies per won war, Long Night | — | +1 | +3 | +3 |
| Extra duchies per won war, Winter Is Coming | — | — | +1 | +3 |
| White Walkers beside him at the Last Hour | 1 | 2 | 3 | 3 |

On top of every tier the Night King carries a standing command bonus (advantage 12, +20% damage and toughness, dread pinned at maximum), and under *The Cycle* each later wave adds more. The host figures are floors the dead top up to as they conquer — twenty thousand beyond the Wall, fifty once the far north is theirs, a hundred thousand when the Wall falls, two hundred thousand when the North does — scaled by tier and hard-capped at 500,000.

### War tier

*Duchy* — the Others take one duchy at a time: smaller steps, a front line that is harder to read, great seats still falling last. The slowest invasion by a long way, and the most time to answer the call. *March* (the default) — one march at a time, the unit the map is drawn in; a bannerman's seat always falls before his liege's, and Winterfell only once the North has gone quiet. Against the small realms beyond the Wall the March setting works a duchy at a time, so the far north does not grind. *Empire* — fewer, vaster wars that sweep entire realms at once.

### Resolution

*Death* (default) — the Long Night ends the moment the leader of the Others dies; kill him and the dark lifts, wherever the war stands. *War for the Dawn* — killing the Night King is not enough: another of the Others takes the crown and the dark goes on until the War for the Dawn is won. Either way the ending happens on screen, with the worthiest champion chosen to strike the final blow — and on both settings only a bane destroys him.

### The Cycle

With *Recurrence* set to The Cycle, the Dawn is not the end. The Wall is raised anew, the veterans of the first dawn are known by name in the record, and the second night comes heavier than the first — each wave adds advantage to the Night King who leads it.

## The prophecy

| Rule | Options |
|---|---|
| **Was There Ever a Prince?** | **Let the Flames Decide** (default — a hidden coin, tossed once at game start and never shown) · There Is a Prince · There Is No Prince |
| **Naming** | **As the Books Have It** (default — 2 marks to be Favored, 4 to be Chosen) · The Flames Are Loud (1 and 3) · The Citadel's Reading (3 and 6) · Nobody Is Ever Named |
| **Claimants** | **Anyone at All** (default) · Players Only · One at a Time (at most one living Chosen; the seat reopens on death) |

Under *There Is No Prince*, every naming is a red priest reading his own hopes, and the world finds that out at the end. Under *Nobody Is Ever Named*, the Favored and Chosen traits stay unobtainable, as they were in AGOT before this mod.
